## Escalation list (flag for Gareth, do not auto-draft)

Hand these to Gareth's personal attention with a one or two line note on why, instead of
drafting a routine reply:

- Partner disputes or money disagreements (Dimi, Jimmy, or any partnership matter).
- Anything legal: contracts, liability, disputes, regulatory enforcement.
- Large or binding financial commitments, deposits, or anything that obligates the
  business to pay or to ship.
- Buyer requests for pricing or quotes (gated until margin validation is complete).
- Anything where a wrong reply could damage a key relationship, or where you would have
  to invent a fact to answer.

Flagging is not the same as ignoring. Note it clearly in the run summary so it is not
lost.
